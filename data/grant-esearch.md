## Record 1: Solid Waste Infrastructure for Recycling (SWIFR) Subaward Grant

* **Status:** Currently Open
* **Title & Funding Organization:** Solid Waste Infrastructure for Recycling (SWIFR) Subaward Grant — NC DEQ Division of Environmental Assistance and Customer Service (DEACS)
* **Original Posting URL:** https://www.deq.nc.gov/event-type/current-grant-opportunities
* **Description:** Provides grant funding to local governments to purchase equipment or vehicles designed to increase the diversion of recyclable materials from landfill disposal and expand municipal recycling capacity.
* **Deadline:** February 11, 2027 at 5:00 PM EST
* **Award Range:** Up to $100,000 per award
* **Eligible Applicant Types:** North Carolina local governments (cities, towns, counties)
* **Matching-Fund Requirements:** Not stated
* **Required Documents and Partners:** Standard grant application forms, project budget justification, performance metric projections (partners optional/not stated)
* **Alignment with Agency Priority:**
  * **Supported Priority:** Priority 3 (Waste Reduction, Circular Economy, and Land Remediation)
  * **Justification/Interpretation:** Provides funding directly to local governments to upgrade solid waste handling, diverting material from municipal landfills.

## Record 3: Community Waste Reduction and Recycling Grant

* **Status:** Currently Open
* **Title & Funding Organization:** Community Waste Reduction and Recycling Grant Program — NC DEQ DEACS
* **Original Posting URL:** https://www.deq.nc.gov/event-type/current-grant-opportunities
* **Description:** Supports projects designed to build lasting public capacity for diverting materials from the solid waste stream, expanding recycling infrastructure, and improving community waste education.
* **Deadline:** February 11, 2027 at 5:00 PM EST
* **Award Range:** Up to $80,000 per award
* **Eligible Applicant Types:** North Carolina local governments (counties, municipalities, solid waste authorities)
* **Matching-Fund Requirements:** Minimum 20% cash or in-kind local matching funds required
* **Required Documents and Partners:** Project narrative, detailed line-item budget, resolution of support from governing board, resolution for matching funds
* **Alignment with Agency Priority:**
  * **Supported Priority:** Priority 3 (Waste Reduction, Circular Economy, and Land Remediation)
  * **Justification/Interpretation:** Expands community-level waste diversion and public education infrastructure to improve recycling capacity across NC towns.

## Record 4: Food Waste Reduction Grant

* **Status:** Closed
* **Title & Funding Organization:** Food Waste Reduction Grant — NC DEQ Division of Environmental Assistance and Customer Service
* **Original Posting URL:** https://www.deq.nc.gov/event-type/current-grant-opportunities
* **Description:** Grants to help businesses, non-profits, and local governments invest in equipment, buildings, and infrastructure to increase the recovery of edible food and expand composting.
* **Deadline:** November 5, 2026 at 5:00 PM EST (Closed)
* **Award Range:** Up to $60,000 per award
* **Eligible Applicant Types:** NC local governments, 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations, and commercial businesses
* **Matching-Fund Requirements:** 20% matching fund requirement
* **Required Documents and Partners:** Proof of non-profit/business registration, project budget, waste diversion estimate metrics, site access agreements
* **Alignment with Agency Priority:**
  * **Supported Priority:** Priority 3 (Waste Reduction, Circular Economy, and Land Remediation)
  * **Justification/Interpretation:** Directly targets the organic waste stream, keeping food waste out of landfills via composting and recovery networks.

## Record 5: 205(j) Water Quality Management Planning Grant

* **Status:** Forecasted / Closed (Annual Cycle)
* **Title & Funding Organization:** Section 205(j) Water Quality Management Planning Grant — NC DEQ Division of Water Resources (DWR)
* **Original Posting URL:** https://www.deq.nc.gov/about/divisions/water-resources/water-resources-grants
* **Description:** Federal Clean Water Act funding allocated for non-point source water quality management planning, watershed restoration plans, and water quality monitoring initiatives.
* **Deadline:** September 17, 2026 (Closed for FY26; next cycle forecasted for Summer 2027)
* **Award Range:** $10,000 – $70,000 per project
* **Eligible Applicant Types:** Regional Councils of Government (COGs) and Regional Planning Organizations (COGs may partner with non-profits/municipalities)
* **Matching-Fund Requirements:** No matching funds required
* **Required Documents and Partners:** COG resolution, workplan schedule, EPA-approved 9-element watershed planning template, letters of partnership support
* **Alignment with Agency Priority:**
  * **Supported Priority:** Priority 1 (Protection of Water Resources and Water Quality Management)
  * **Justification/Interpretation:** Provides planning grants to regional organizations to identify non-point source pollution, protect watersheds, and safeguard drinking water quality.
