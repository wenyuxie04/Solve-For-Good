# Agency Profile: North Carolina Department of Environmental Quality (NC DEQ)

**Official Strategic Plan Source:** [NC DEQ Strategic Plan 2025–2029 (OSBM)](https://www.osbm.nc.gov/strategic-plan-deq/open)

---

### Strategic Priorities

#### Priority 1: Protection of Water Resources and Water Quality Management
* **Description:** Focuses on advancing water quality management strategies beyond traditional limits, targeting toxins, nutrients, pathogens, and "forever chemicals" (PFAS) in surface water, groundwater, and drinking water sources.
* **Keywords:** Water Quality, PFAS, Toxins, Nutrients, Watershed, Public Health
* **Source:** [NC DEQ Strategic Plan 2025–2029 (Strategy 1.A & 3.A)](https://www.osbm.nc.gov/strategic-plan-deq/open)

#### Priority 2: Community Climate Resilience and Flood Mitigation
* **Description:** Building statewide capacity to withstand natural hazards, climate impacts, and extreme weather events by integrating resilience planning into long-term recovery, supporting regional flood blueprinting, and deploying natural infrastructure.
* **Keywords:** Resilience, Flood Mitigation, Natural Hazards, Climate Adaptation, Recovery
* **Source:** [NC DEQ Strategic Plan 2025–2029 (Goal 2)](https://www.osbm.nc.gov/strategic-plan-deq/open)

#### Priority 3: Waste Reduction, Circular Economy, and Land Remediation
* **Description:** Reducing waste stream volume through solid waste infrastructure, recycling business growth, food waste recovery, and Brownfields redevelopment to promote sustainable land reuse and economic development.
* **Keywords:** Solid Waste, Recycling, Food Waste, Brownfields, Waste Diversion, Sustainability
* **Source:** [NC DEQ Strategic Plan 2025–2029 (Strategy 1.C & 1.E)](https://www.osbm.nc.gov/strategic-plan-deq/open)

#### Priority 4: Energy Efficiency and Clean Energy Deployment
* **Description:** Reducing greenhouse gas emissions and energy burdens through building energy efficiency upgrades, weatherization assistance, clean energy deployment, and expanding zero-emission vehicle infrastructure.
* **Keywords:** Energy Efficiency, Clean Energy, Weatherization, Building Upgrades, Zero-Emission
* **Source:** [NC DEQ 2022/2025 Climate Strategy Reports](https://www.deq.nc.gov/ncdncr-climate-strategy-report-2025/open)

#### Priority 5: Infrastructure Support for Distressed Communities
* **Description:** Directing environmental infrastructure grants, planning grants, and technical outreach to designated "Distressed" local government units to build long-term operational viability and public infrastructure safety.
* **Keywords:** Infrastructure, Distressed Communities, Capacity Building, Local Government, Grants
* **Source:** [NC DEQ Strategic Plan 2025–2029 (Strategy 4.C)](https://www.osbm.nc.gov/strategic-plan-deq/open)
## 3. Agricultural Development and Farmland Preservation Grant
- **Agency:** NCDA&CS
- **Target Area:** Farmland Conservation Easements
- **Priority Alignment:** Farmland Preservation & Agribusiness
- **Eligibility:** Local Governments, Nonprofit Land Trusts

## 4. Rural Transformation Grant Fund
- **Agency:** NC Commerce
- **Target Area:** Downtown Revitalization & Infrastructure
- **Priority Alignment:** Rural Infrastructure & Economic Development
- **Eligibility:** Rural Local Governments

## 5. Parks and Recreation Trust Fund (PARTF)
- **Agency:** NC DNCR
- **Target Area:** Public Park Acquisition & Facility Renovation
- **Priority Alignment:** Outdoor Recreation & Conservation
- **Eligibility:** NC Counties and Municipalities
